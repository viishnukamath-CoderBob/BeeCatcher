How to play

Tap the bee

Tap or click the bee before it flies away. Missing costs 10 points.

⚡

Build a streak

Every 3 catches in a row raises your multiplier, up to 5×. A miss resets it.

🚀

10 levels

Catch enough bees to clear each level. Every level adds bonus time and gets faster and trickier.

🌟

Golden bees

Golden bees are worth triple points and add extra seconds to the clock.

🚫

Avoid decoys

Dark bees with a red ring are decoys. Tapping one costs 100 points.

🏆

Win swag

Your final score sets your tier, and your tier sets your prize.
